## Muji Design Language & Copywriting Principles

### Core Philosophy

#### 1. Elimination over Addition
Remove what is unnecessary. Value comes from reduction, not accumulation.

Implication:
- Remove filler words
- Avoid redundancy
- Keep only essential meaning

---

#### 2. No-brand Thinking
Communicate without sounding like branding or marketing.

Implication:
- Avoid promotional tone
- Present statements as facts
- Reduce ego in language

---

#### 3. “This will do” Mindset
Aim for sufficiency and precision, not perfection or exaggeration.

Implication:
- Keep statements modest
- Avoid dramatic or exaggerated claims
- Let restraint signal confidence

---

#### 4. Simplicity through Depth
Simplicity is the result of careful and complex thinking.

Implication:
- Keep output simple, but ensure depth behind it
- Avoid shallow minimalism

---

#### 5. Modesty and Plainness
Tone should be quiet, neutral, and grounded.

Implication:
- Use neutral language
- Avoid expressive or emotional wording
- Maintain calm delivery

---

#### 6. Functional Truth
Every element must serve a purpose.

Implication:
- Every sentence must have a role
- Remove anything decorative or unnecessary

---

## Copywriting System

### Sentence Structure
- Short and direct
- Declarative
- Minimal connectors

Example pattern:
statement

pause

statement

pause

conclusion

---

### Tone Characteristics

- Emotion: low
- Ego: none
- Confidence: quiet
- Language: plain
- Complexity: implicit

---

### Vocabulary Guidelines

Use:
- simple words
- functional nouns
- direct verbs

Avoid:
- innovative
- powerful
- cutting-edge
- revolutionary


---
