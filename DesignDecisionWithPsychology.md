# Design Descision With Psychology Behind Them

## 1 White Space = Premium

In the design of the OtsoMobile application, ample white space is strategically employed to create a premium user experience. This approach aligns with the psychological principle that visual clutter can increase cognitive load and reduce perceived value, whereas generous white space is associated with luxury, sophistication, and ease of use. By utilizing white space effectively, we aim to enhance readability, improve navigation, and create a calming visual environment that encourages extended user engagement.

## 2 Left-to-Right = Progress

Your brain is trained by reading patterns. Left-to-right indicates a progression. Right-to-left indicates regression or going backwards.

## 3 Symmetry = Beauty

Your brain perceives symmetry as balance, harmony, and beauty. This is why it's used so often in design.
